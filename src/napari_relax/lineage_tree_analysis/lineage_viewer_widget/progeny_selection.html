This widget focuses on navigation of the embryo using the Viewer and the Lineage Viewer.

  • The Lineage Viewer is interactive: Left clicking on nodes will select a cell and its sublineage. on the Lineage Viewer and the Napari Viewer
  • Double left click will also move the Napari Viewer to the spawn time of the selected cell.
  • The lineage loaded currently can be changed by using the slider at the bottom of the Lineage Viewer.
  • The user can also Shift-Right click on the Viewer to select cells.
  • After selecting a cell, the user can click on Select Sub/Lineage to choose its lineage or sublineage and see it on both the Napari Viewer and the Lineage Viewer.

Distance Calculation

Clustermap
  • Use the slider to navigate through the different levels of comparison
  • Choose the normalization (max, sum or None) and the colormap
  • Hover over the clustermap to see the compared lineages and their score
  • Click on the clustermap:
    • To project colors to the lineages being compared, on the Napari viewer
    • Show the sub/-lineages being compared on the tree graph on top
    • Tick Move in time to also move the viewer to their timepoint
  • Save the comparisons calculated. (The comparisons are also kept on the LineageTree object, thus there is the option to resave the object)

Full documentation: guignardlab.github.io/napari-relax
